# Privacy Policy for ChronoFuel

**Last Updated:** September, 2026

AbrSoft ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ChronoFuel mobile application and its associated Wear OS companion app (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect minimal information to provide and improve our App. ChronoFuel is designed to work primarily offline and stores most of its data locally on your device.

### Local Data & Permissions
*   **Camera Permission:** The App requests access to your device's camera to allow you to take photos (e.g., documenting faults or events during a flight). These photos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
*   **Wear OS Sync (Bluetooth/Network):** The App communicates with its Wear OS companion app using the Google Play Services Wearable Data Layer. Data exchanged between the phone and watch (such as fuel calculations and timer state) is transmitted locally and is not sent to our servers.

### Analytics and Crash Reporting
We use third-party services, specifically **Firebase Crashlytics** and **Google Analytics**, to collect crash reports and anonymous usage data. This helps us identify bugs and improve the stability of the App. 
*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** Collects data such as device state information, unique device identifiers, and crash traces.
*   You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting their privacy policy: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 2. Data Retention and Deletion
Your flight records, photos, and fuel calculations are stored in the App's local database on your device. You can delete this data at any time by deleting individual flight records within the App or by uninstalling the App entirely.

## 3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
